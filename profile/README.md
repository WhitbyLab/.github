# WhitbyLab

This organization hosts the viral genomics and assembly pipelines for the Viral Oncology Section (VOS), built 
for reproducibility and reuse.

The VOS, also known as the Whitby Laboratory, is part of the 
AIDS and Cancer Virus Program (ACVP) at the Frederick National Laboratory for Cancer 
Research.

We study human gammaherpesviruses, specifically Kaposi sarcoma herpesvirus (KSHV or HHV-8) and their role in cancer.

Learn more:
